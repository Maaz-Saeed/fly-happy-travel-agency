# User Manual (Customer Guide)

## 1. Creating an Account
1. Click **Login** in the top navigation, then **Register here**.
2. Fill in your full name, email, phone, CNIC (optional) and a password (minimum 6 characters).
3. Submit the form and log in with your new credentials.

## 2. Browsing Destinations, Airlines & Packages
- Use the **Destinations** page to search by city/country, filter by region or visa requirement.
- Click any destination card to see full details: airport, flight duration, weather, best season, available packages, hotels and top airlines.
- Browse **Airlines** and **Packages** pages the same way; use category filters on Packages (Holiday, Family, Corporate, Group, Umrah, Hajj).

## 3. Booking a Flight
1. Click **Book Now** in the navigation (or "Select & Book" on any destination).
2. Choose your airline, trip type (One Way / Round Trip), travel dates, number of adults/children/infants, seat class and meal preference.
3. Fill in passenger details (name, passport number, CNIC, phone, email) — these are pre-filled from your profile if available.
4. Watch the **Estimated Total** update live as you change passengers/seat class/trip type.
5. Click **Continue to Payment**. Your booking is saved with a unique **Booking ID** and **Ticket Number**, status `Pending Payment`.

## 4. Paying for Your Booking
1. On the Payment page, choose a method: Visa/MasterCard/Debit Card, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer or Cash.
2. Enter the relevant details (card info, mobile account number) — this is a **simulated** payment for demonstration.
3. Submit. Card/wallet/bank payments confirm your booking immediately; Cash leaves it pending until you visit our office.
4. You'll receive a **Receipt** immediately, and a full **Invoice** is available any time from your dashboard.

## 5. Managing Your Bookings
Go to **My Account → My Bookings** to:
- View all bookings and filter by status (Pending/Confirmed/Completed/Cancelled).
- Open a booking to see its full ticket/confirmation details.
- Print the invoice for a confirmed booking.
- Cancel a booking that hasn't already been cancelled or completed.

## 6. Payment History
**My Account → Payment History** lists every payment you've made, with receipt numbers, methods, and statuses — click **Receipt** to view/print any of them.

## 7. Updating Your Profile & Password
- **My Account → Update Profile** — edit your name, phone, CNIC, passport number and address.
- **My Account → Change Password** — enter your current password plus a new one twice to confirm.

## 8. Getting Help
- **Chat Widget** (bottom-left bubble) — ask about office timings, visa process, passport requirements, booking steps, refund policy, payment methods, insurance, destinations, or contact details, any time.
- **Contact Page** — send a message via the form, view our office location on the map, or reach us by phone/WhatsApp/email.
- **My Account → Contact Support** — submit a support request directly from your dashboard while logged in.

## 9. Logging Out
Click **Logout** in the navigation or your dashboard sidebar at any time.
